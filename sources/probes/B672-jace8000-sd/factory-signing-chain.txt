=== CertISW header of n4-titan-am335x.signed: TI GP-format, NOT X.509 ===
No ASN.1 SEQUENCE (0x30 0x82) anywhere in the first 0x400 of the header; the region around the cert-length
field (0x350) is high-entropy (e.g. 0x340..: b6fd95ca3e5b418a...) = a raw hash/signature, not a DER cert.
Conclusion: the CertISW wrapper is a TI GP (general-purpose) image header with a raw hash/signature block, NOT
an HS X.509 certificate chain. The decodable certificate chain for the payload is the JAR-level NIAGARA4.RSA
above. (Full TI-GP header field decode = out of scope; the "is there an X.509 chain in the boot image" question
is answered: no.)
